# Contributing

Thank you for helping shape The Cloud Ownership Manifesto.

## Sign it

1. Fork this repo.
2. Add one row at the end of the table in [SIGNATORIES.md](SIGNATORIES.md): your name, your role and your company.
3. Open a pull request titled "Sign: <your name>".

A signature is personal; it does not commit your employer.

## Propose a change

- **To discuss an idea**, open an issue: what is wrong or missing, and an example from real work.
- **To change wording**, open a pull request against [PRINCIPLES.md](PRINCIPLES.md) and explain why in the description.
- Principles change through pull requests, reviewed by DevSecOps.cz as maintainers.
- The manifesto itself (values and non-negotiables) changes at most once a year, after a public review. Proposals for it are collected as issues until then.

Accepted changes are recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md), with credit to the people who proposed them.

## Ground rules

Be specific, be kind, and argue from real work. Sales pitches and promotion are closed without discussion.
